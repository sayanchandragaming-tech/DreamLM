Research workspace

DreamLM features

Tools available in the current DreamLM application.

Scientific conversations

Send questions and receive responses through the DreamLM assistant. The workspace includes example prompts spanning general relativity, machine-learning physics, quantum information, and differential geometry.

Mathematical input and conversation tools

  • Insert common mathematical symbols from a glyph shelf.
  • Review and continue previous conversations stored in the browser.
  • Copy message text, retry a response, and rename, export, or delete a conversation.
  • Export conversations as Markdown or plain text.

Workspace preferences

Choose among the Scientific Elegance, Deep Enclave, and Ivory Monograph appearance themes. Email and Google sign-in options are available on the access screen.

Availability

DreamLM is presented as a private beta. Interactive workspace access is restricted to authorized users.
