About DreamLM

DreamLM is an AI assistant developed by Dream Circuit for theoretical reasoning, computational physics, and mathematical synthesis.

Who we are

The DreamLM application identifies Arindam Roy and Sayan Chandra as its creators and founders. The public application describes Dream Circuit as the developer of DreamLM.

Current availability

DreamLM is currently presented as a private beta research workspace. Access to the interactive workspace is restricted; these information pages are public.

The repository does not document a public launch date.
