Website use

DreamLM terms

These terms describe basic expectations for using the DreamLM website and its private beta workspace.

Access

The interactive DreamLM workspace is currently a restricted private beta. Use it only if you have been authorized to access it, and keep your sign-in credentials under your control.

Acceptable use

Do not use the website to interfere with its operation, attempt access you have not been granted, or submit material in violation of applicable rules or another person’s rights. Access may be limited or withdrawn.

AI responses

Responses are generated by an AI assistant and may be incomplete or incorrect. Review and independently verify outputs before relying on them, especially for research, calculations, or consequential decisions.

Availability and changes

The website and beta features may change or be unavailable. The repository does not document an availability guarantee or a public launch date.

Data handling

Messages are stored in the browser and sent to the configured response service when submitted. See the privacy page for the data handling described by the current application.
